How to play

You see the result of somebody’s first Wordle guess. The clue is specially chosen: among every accepted Wordle word, only one solution fits.

Keep guessing until you find it. Wrong answers do not reveal any extra tiles.

To install, use the Install button when offered. On iPhone, open this page in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen.
